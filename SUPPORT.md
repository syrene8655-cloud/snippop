# Support

For general questions, check the [user guide](https://snippop.cc/en/guide/) first.

## Bug reports

[Open an issue](https://github.com/syrene8655-cloud/snippop/issues/new) and include:

- SnipPop version and build number
- macOS version and Mac model/chip
- What you were trying to do
- Steps to reproduce the problem
- What you expected and what happened instead
- A redacted screenshot or recording, if useful

## Feature requests

Describe the task you want to complete, how you do it now, and how the proposed change would help. Search existing issues before opening a new one.

## Purchase and activation

Email **syrene8655@gmail.com** for purchase, activation, license recovery, or refund questions. Do not post license keys, payment details, or receipts in public issues.
