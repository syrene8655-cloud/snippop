# SnipPop

**Keep your Mac screenshots within reach.**

SnipPop turns saved screenshots into floating previews you can pin, annotate, copy, save, or drag into another app. It is a native Mac screenshot companion built around the screenshots you already take.

[Download & try SnipPop](https://snippop.cc/en/#download) · [User guide](https://snippop.cc/en/guide/) · [Watch the demo](https://youtu.be/2xNPBg-QZcY) · [中文说明](README.zh-CN.md)

## What this repository is for

This is the public home for SnipPop documentation, release notes, bug reports, and feature requests. **SnipPop is a commercial, closed-source app. This repository does not contain the app's source code or installers.** Download the official app from [snippop.cc](https://snippop.cc/en/).

## What you can do

- Keep screenshots in floating bubbles and pin the ones you need.
- Crop and annotate with arrows, shapes, and text; pixelate sensitive areas.
- Extract text with on-device OCR.
- Resize images and compress PNG/JPEG files, including batch processing.
- Copy, save, or drag an image into another app.
- Preview completed screen recordings saved by macOS and drag them into your workflow.

SnipPop works with screenshots saved as files, including those taken with **Shift–Command–3, 4, or 5**. Clipboard-only screenshots do not automatically become bubbles. It can display up to 14 image/video previews, including pinned items. Closing a bubble does not delete the original file.

## Requirements

- Apple silicon Mac
- macOS 13 or later
- Interface languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, and Korean

The current download is for Apple silicon; there is no Intel build.

## Privacy

Screenshot editing and OCR run locally on your Mac. SnipPop does not upload your screenshots for processing or cloud sync. Checkout and licensing use online services. If you share an image with another app, that app's own policies apply. Read the [privacy policy](https://snippop.cc/en/privacy/).
